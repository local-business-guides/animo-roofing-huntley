How to review a replacement proposal

A replacement project may involve more than selecting a new roofing material. Ask the contractor to explain what preparation is planned, which sections are included, how the work will be organized, and what cleanup is part of the job. If there are choices to make, ask for the differences in plain terms and take time to consider them.

Before you decide, check that you can answer these questions:
- What specific work does the written proposal describe?
- Which materials or options have been presented?
- What should be moved or kept clear around the home?
- How will schedule updates and questions be handled?
- What conditions could change the proposed scope, and how will those be discussed?

Avoid comparing proposals by looking only at a single line or material name. The scope and exclusions matter too. Don't agree to work you don't understand, and don't climb onto the roof to check its condition yourself. If you see an active leak or another concern, explain what is happening and ask a professional how to proceed.
