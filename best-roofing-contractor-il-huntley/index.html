What to ask and have ready

The roofing work to consider depends on the roof and the concern, so ask the contractor to explain the scope in plain language. Before the conversation, gather any details that can help describe the situation, such as when you noticed the issue and which part of the home it affects. If you have photos you took safely from the ground, keep them handy.

A short checklist can keep the discussion focused:

  • What issue do you see, and what work are you recommending?
  • What parts of the roof are included in the proposed scope?
  • How will you explain any additional work that may come up?
  • What should I do before work begins?
  • Who should I contact if I have a question about the work?

Avoid assuming that every roof concern calls for the same solution. Don’t agree to work you don’t understand, and don’t put yourself at risk by climbing onto the roof to gather details. If you see a change that concerns you, contact a roofing professional to discuss it.
