Start with a clear roofing plan

A Roofing Contractor Huntley IL homeowners consider should be able to explain what they think needs attention and what the next steps involve. Before you agree to work, find out whether the discussion is about a specific concern, a broader roof project, or simply determining what needs to be done. Ask what the proposed work includes, what it does not include, and how you’ll know when the work is complete.

A few questions can help you compare your options:

  • What parts of the roof are included in the proposed scope?
  • What materials or work methods are being considered?
  • What access to the property will be needed?
  • How will timing and any changes to the plan be communicated?
  • What should you do if you notice a new concern before work begins?

Getting clear answers is more useful than choosing based on a brief description alone. Take notes so you can compare the details later.

What to clarify about the work
