Prepare for a useful conversation

You don’t need to diagnose a roof problem before calling. Give the contractor a concise account of what you’ve observed and avoid guessing about the cause. If you have photos taken from a safe location, you can refer to them while explaining what caught your attention. Never climb onto the roof to take pictures or check a concern yourself.

Keep this checklist nearby when you speak with a contractor:

  • What do you recommend based on the concern I described?
  • Which parts of the roof are included in the proposed work?
  • What is not included in that scope?
  • How should I raise questions if something is unclear?
  • What should I prepare before the work begins?

A common mistake is to compare contractors using only a short description or a single detail. Instead, write down what each person says the work covers and note anything that still needs clarification. Take time to review the explanation before making a decision.
