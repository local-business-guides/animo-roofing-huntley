What a new roof installation covers

The exact work depends on the home and project. A useful discussion with the roofer can cover the proposed roofing materials, preparation of the work area, how the installation will proceed, and what cleanup is included. Ask who will explain any changes if the roof conditions differ from what was expected.

Before agreeing to a plan, you can make a short checklist:
- Confirm which parts of the roof are included in the project.
- Ask what material choices are available and how they differ.
- Find out how the work area and nearby access points should be prepared.
- Ask how the schedule will be communicated and who to contact with questions.
- Review the written scope so you know what is and is not included.

Move vehicles or outdoor items only after discussing where crews may need access. Keep children and pets away from the active work area, and let the roofer know about access needs in advance. Avoid choosing materials based only on appearance. Confirm that the proposed option suits the project and that its details are written down.
