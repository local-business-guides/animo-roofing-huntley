What to prepare and ask about

Roof Maintenance Huntley can begin with a short list of observations and questions. Jot down what you've noticed, where it appears to be, and whether it has changed. If you have photos taken safely from the ground, keep them handy, but don't put yourself at risk to get a closer view.

Before calling, consider this checklist:

Avoid treating a quick look from indoors or the ground as a complete roof assessment. Don't walk on the roof or attempt a repair if you aren't equipped to do so.
